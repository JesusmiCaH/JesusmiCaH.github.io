## Initial prompt

Asset type: square 1:1 website favicon and 8-bit profile avatar.
Input images: Image 1 is the canonical Tommy virtual-persona character design reference (multiple poses of the SAME character); Image 2 is the original identity sketch. Use only as identity references, not as edit canvases.
Primary request: Generate ONE original straight-on, front-facing head-and-shoulders pixel-art portrait of this same character, centered in a square canvas, looking directly at viewer with a small friendly confident smile.
Identity: light warm tan/brown bobbed hair, one lively upward forelock, two tan fox/cat ears, black rectangular glasses, big dark amber eyes with tiny restrained cyan highlights, warm fair skin, charcoal grey jacket, pale shirt and small red collar accent. Preserve this recognizable persona. Crop below shoulders; prioritize face, glasses and ears, no hands, tail or extra props.
Style: authentic 8-bit game portrait, deliberate coarse square pixels on a consistent approximately 32x32 pixel design grid, enlarged cleanly with hard nearest-neighbor edges, limited harmonious palette, simple readable silhouettes and facial features. NO smooth outlines, gradients, blur, anti-aliasing, microtexture or realistic rendering. Should read clearly in a 16 or 32 pixel browser tab.
Composition: symmetric frontal head, head fills most of square, intact ears and forelock with a small safe margin, minimal shoulders at bottom. Actual transparent background outside the silhouette, preserve real alpha; no grey contact-sheet background, no checkerboard painted into image. Thin pale pixel outline may separate dark silhouette from browser chrome.
Constraints: exactly one portrait only; no text, letters, logo, watermark, sparkles, extra characters, grids, mockup, interface, circle badge or collage. Square aspect ratio 1:1.
