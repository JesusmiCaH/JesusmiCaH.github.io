# Tommy mascot — canonical character guide

This folder is the durable visual reference for Chenghao “Tommy” Jiang's personal-site mascot. Future icons, reaction stickers, illustrations, animation, and 3D interpretations should begin here.

## Reference hierarchy

1. `tommy-mascot-concept-v1.png` — approved rendering direction and canonical level of polish.
2. `friend-sketch-reference.png` — original identity sketch; use it to recover character-specific details when needed.

The approved concept is a style reference, not a finished web asset. Its grey contact-sheet background must not appear in exported stickers or icons.

## Identity invariants

- Light warm-brown bobbed hair with one lively upward forelock.
- Tan fox/cat ears with warm inner-ear shading and a fluffy tan tail.
- Black rectangular glasses.
- Very large dark eyes with warm amber and restrained cyan highlights.
- Grey tailored jacket, light plaid shirt, and a small red collar accent.
- Friendly, curious, clever energy; expressive without becoming manic or childish.
- A tiny white companion may appear as an occasional secondary prop, never as the main character.

Do not casually change the hair color, glasses silhouette, ear shape, eye palette, outfit family, or tail color. Do not add branded logos, captions, watermarks, or unrelated characters.

## Rendering language

- Premium polished chibi illustration with clean black ink contours and a trace of hand-drawn wobble.
- Soft cel shading, warm skin and hair tones, crisp facial readability, and a white sticker rim when used as a sticker.
- Personal-site accent palette: cream paper, charcoal, warm orange-red, fluorescent yellow-green, and dark forest green; cyan or purple may be used sparingly.
- Keep silhouettes readable at small sizes. Avoid overly fine costume detail and excessive glossy rendering.
- Final web stickers require a genuinely transparent background and generous transparent padding.

## Canonical six-piece starter set

1. Favicon: friendly, confident head-and-shoulders portrait with a simple silhouette.
2. Hello: cheerful full-body wave.
3. Field notes: thinking while writing in a small notebook.
4. Eureka: delighted idea moment with one small spark.
5. Game developer: focused expression with a compact controller.
6. Bug found: playful surprise, optionally accompanied by the tiny white companion.

## Future 3D translation

Preserve the head-to-body ratio, glasses, forelock, ears, tail, jacket silhouette, and warm/cyan eye highlights. Translate the ink outline into clean toon-shaded edge treatment rather than pursuing photorealism. Model ears, glasses, forelock, jacket lapels, and tail as separate controllable pieces so the character can support expressive animation and small interactive web scenes.

## Reusable generation seed

Use the two reference images above. Render the same virtual persona as a polished hand-drawn chibi character with clean ink outlines, subtle natural wobble, soft cel shading, and expressive but restrained facial acting. Preserve every identity invariant in this guide. Match the requested pose and medium while keeping the character recognizably identical. Use a genuinely transparent background for standalone web assets; no text, logo, watermark, or extra character unless explicitly requested.
